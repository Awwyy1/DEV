How the map link looks

A small, free link to Google Maps under each photo and each article. No API key, no cost. On a phone it opens the Maps app straight on the spot. Below: where it sits, three styles to choose from, and a live example of an embedded mini-map.

01 — On the photo card (detail page)

Under the location line

The link sits right under “place · coordinates · date”, before the description. Quiet, on-brand, one tap to the map.

03 — In the article

Under the byline

Same link, placed at the top of the piece next to the date, so a reader can jump to the spot before or after reading.
