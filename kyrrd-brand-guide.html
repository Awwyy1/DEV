02 — Logo usage

Do & Don't

The wordmark is quiet by design. Protect it.

Do
  • keep ð in Glacier; the rest in Ink (light) or Snow (dark)
  • always lowercase, generous clear space
  • place on calm light grounds, deep Ink, or a quiet photo edge
  • pair with the seal for stamps, avatars, certificates
Don't
  • recolour ð to anything but Glacier; don't colour the whole word
  • UPPERCASE, stretch, skew, outline or add drop-shadows
  • lay it over a busy / high-contrast part of an image
  • swap the typeface or loosen the tracking

08 — Photography

The image

Cool, high-key, calm. The photo “hangs on the wall” — generous margins, no frames or shadows in the gallery. Previews carry the ð watermark and are reduced; the clean, signed hi-res is delivered only after payment.

Do
  • cool white balance, muted saturation
  • air around the frame, one 4:5 format
  • quiet scenes: mist, ice, water, stone, minimal
  • ð watermark, bottom-right, low opacity
Don't
  • warm “postcard” filters, blown-out sunsets
  • borders, shadows, rounded corners on the image
  • collages — one image = one work
  • large Glacier fills over a photo
